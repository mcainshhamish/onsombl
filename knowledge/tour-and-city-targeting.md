title: Using city data for shows and promotion
source: onsombl starter playbook (example content)

Listener city data tells you where to spend limited time and money.

Rank cities by listeners, then by growth. A mid-sized city growing quickly is often a better first show than the biggest city, because it is cheaper to reach and a good turnout is easier to achieve.

Before announcing a date: post to that city's listeners with a local message, ask your list who wants a show there, and check a few venues of the right size.

Turn live audiences into fans: add a pre-save or follow link to every ticket confirmation email, and have a simple way to join your list at the merch table. People at a show are your most likely long-term fans.
