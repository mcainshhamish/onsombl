Plan three clips per release, each built around one hook: the chorus, a behind-the-scenes moment, or the story of the song.

Put the hook in the first two seconds. Use the same section of the track in every clip so the sound becomes recognisable.

Post across several days, not all at once, and reply to comments in the first hour. Check which clip brings people to your profile and make the next one in the same style.

Treat views as a first step. The goals that matter for a musician are follows, saves and mailing list sign-ups.
