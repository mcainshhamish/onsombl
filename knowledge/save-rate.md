Save rate is saves divided by listeners. It shows how many people wanted to hear a track again, so it is a quality signal that streams alone do not give.

Compare each release with your own catalog average first. Outside benchmarks matter less than whether a release is above or below your usual level.

If a release has a low save rate:
- Check where its listeners came from. Algorithmic listeners often save less than fans.
- Look at the first 30 seconds. A long intro loses casual listeners.
- Add a call to action in your posts: "save this to your library".
- Consider a live or stripped version, which often converts better with existing fans.
