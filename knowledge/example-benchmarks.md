These ranges are placeholders to show how benchmarks will work. Replace them with real data before relying on them.

- Under 10K monthly listeners: focus on finding your first 1,000 true fans. Growth is lumpy and driven by a few playlists or videos.
- 10K to 100K: algorithmic discovery starts to matter. Repeatable releases and a mailing list are the priorities.
- 100K to 1M: touring and press start to pay back. Watch the returning-listener share closely.
- Above 1M: team, budget and brand partnerships become the main levers.

Save rate and growth should be compared with your own history first. Treat outside ranges as a rough guide only.
