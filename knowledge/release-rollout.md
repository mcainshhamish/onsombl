Plan each release around a repeatable timeline.

- 4 weeks out: lock the artwork, a short visual teaser and the pitch story. Open pre-save links.
- At least 7 days before release day: submit the track to Spotify's editorial team through Spotify for Artists. Pitches made after release day miss that window.
- Release week: post three short clips from the track, email your list, and ask your core fans to save and add it to a playlist, not only to stream it.
- 2 to 4 weeks after: send a follow-up (live version, lyric video, remix) so the track gets a second wave.

Cadence: a new single every 6 to 8 weeks keeps algorithmic recommendations fed better than one large drop a year.
