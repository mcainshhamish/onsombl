---
title: Fan engagement and direct-to-fan basics
source: onsombl starter playbook (example content)
type: playbook
tags: fans, comments, newsletter, mailing list, community, replies, loyalty
---
Reply to comments quickly, especially from people asking about shows or sharing how a song affected them. It costs little and builds loyalty.

Collect emails from day one. A mailing list is the one audience you own, and it does not depend on any platform's algorithm. Offer something small: early access to a track, a demo, or a behind-the-scenes note.

Send a short update every two to four weeks. Keep it personal and use one clear ask, such as save the new single or buy a ticket.

Track who comes back. Returning listeners are a stronger sign of a real fanbase than a large number of new listeners.
