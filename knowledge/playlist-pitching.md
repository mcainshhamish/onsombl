Two routes, different rules.

Editorial (Spotify): use the pitch form in Spotify for Artists, at least 7 days before release. Describe the song in one specific sentence, name the mood and genre, and say what you are doing to promote it.

Independent curators: build a short list of 20 to 30 curators who already play artists like you. Send one personal, short message with a single streaming link and one line on why it fits their playlist. Follow up once after a week, then stop.

What to measure: playlist adds, how many streams come from playlists, and the save rate of listeners from each playlist. Playlists with strong saves are worth thanking and staying close to.
