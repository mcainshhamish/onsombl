Use age and loyalty data to choose who a campaign speaks to.

Find the largest age band and write creative for it: the tone, the platform and the references. Do not try to speak to everyone.

New versus returning listeners: a high share of new listeners means your music is spreading, but only if some of them come back. A low returning share suggests focusing on follow-up content, a mailing list or a save prompt.

Growth in a younger band often comes from short video, while older bands respond better to email, live shows and press. Test one change at a time so you know what worked.
